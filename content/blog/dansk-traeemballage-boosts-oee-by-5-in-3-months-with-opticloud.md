## At a Glance

Dansk Træemballage implemented Opticloud on one production line in 2024 and saw an average **5% increase in OEE within three months**.

## How Opticloud Is Used

Opticloud is used to collect:

- uptime data
- OEE data
- downtime reasons
- waste percentage on the production line

That gives management information that was not previously available.

Operators also use a tablet-based view to monitor uptime directly, which has encouraged faster recovery after stoppages.

## The Result

The setup has given Dansk Træemballage:

- a stronger tool for continuous improvement
- better management insight
- faster operational follow-up after stops
- an average **5% OEE increase** in just three months

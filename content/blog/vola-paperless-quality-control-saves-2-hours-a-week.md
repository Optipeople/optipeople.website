At Vola, quality is a cornerstone. That means production has to run efficiently and profitably, and management needs to be able to choose the right improvements and investments.

## The challenge

Quality checks used to be done on paper, then reviewed and typed in by hand. Scrapped parts were counted by hand in red boxes and logged in a separate Excel sheet. When the report reached management, much of it was guesswork.

## The solution

Today every machine in the CNC department runs digital quality control. Large screens call for each check so it is not forgotten, and operators can see how long it is until the next one. Checks, item numbers and orders are recorded digitally with a timestamp and the operator's name. All run time is tied to an order number, and scrapped parts are counted in the system.

## The result

- Vola saves **about 2 hours a week**, because checks no longer have to be reviewed on paper and typed in by hand.
- Management reporting rests on facts.
- New KPIs: response time on checks (share on time and backlog) and a production analysis with stop causes, uptime and changeover time per machine.
- A report portal that filters and sorts by machine, order, item number, operator and period, and exports to PDF and Excel.

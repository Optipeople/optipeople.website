## At a Glance

Carl Hansen & Søn uses Opticloud to create more accurate production insight and is piloting OptiAI to reduce setup times through better production sequencing.

## How Opticloud Is Used

Opticloud provides reliable data on machinery performance, which gives the company a stronger basis for strategic decisions and operational analysis.

Operators support that work by logging downtime reasons, making it easier to identify where productivity can be improved.

Operators also select the specific item being produced, allowing cycle times to be adjusted accordingly in Opticloud. That creates more accurate production data and supports more precise operational follow-up.

## The OptiAI Pilot

Carl Hansen is also piloting OptiAI to optimize production order sequencing.

The goal is to reduce setup times by suggesting a more efficient order sequence and minimizing transitions between production orders.

## The Result

Together, the setup is intended to give Carl Hansen:

- more accurate productivity insight
- better targeting of improvement areas
- more precise cycle-time handling
- reduced setup times
- higher overall productivity

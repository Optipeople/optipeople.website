In many companies, too much knowledge gets lost between shifts. The handover is short, and there is often a lot to pass on.

The operator panel in OptiPeople Data Platform makes it easier to hand over from one shift to the next.

## What the operator panel helps with

The panel shows a clear list of stop causes from the last shift. That gives the next operator something concrete to start from, so they can see:

- what problems there have been
- whether the problem is solved
- whether help is already on the way

Operators can also enter the stop cause themselves. That makes the handover more useful, and it no longer depends only on what people remember or have time to say.

## Why it makes a difference

You avoid doing the same work twice. If someone has already called maintenance, the next shift doesn't need to report the same problem again.

The handover gets simpler, because it is built on:

- stop causes
- how long the stops lasted
- the notes in the logbook

That saves time, and the next shift can take over without needless confusion.

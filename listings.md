# Where to get OptiPeople listed

Why: AI engines (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini) and Google's "best OEE software" results quote review sites, directories and lists. OptiPeople is on none of them today, so AI answers name Evocon, Factbird, Trendlog and Vetech instead. See `Docs/seo/targets.md` section 4.

Draft texts for Capterra, the review request email, the list pitches and the trade media pitches are on the branch `human-get-optipeople-listed-where-ai`, in `Docs/seo/outreach/`. Fill in the [brackets] (pricing wording, founding year, contacts) before sending.

Use the same facts everywhere: name, one-line description, address, website, logo and categories. AI engines trust a company more when the details match across sites.

Tick a box when the profile is live, and put the date and the profile link after it.

## 1. Review sites (do first)

These are the sites AI engines quote most for "best X software". Reviews from customers matter more than the profile itself.

- [ ] **Capterra, GetApp and Software Advice** (one vendor account at Gartner Digital Markets covers all three). A free basic listing. Categories: OEE software, manufacturing software, MES. Factbird has 12 reviews there.
- [ ] **G2** (g2.com). A free vendor profile. Categories: OEE, MES, manufacturing execution, IIoT.
- [ ] **TrustRadius** (trustradius.com). A free vendor profile.
- [ ] **SourceForge and Slashdot** (one listing covers both). A free listing.
- [ ] **Crozdesk** and **SaaSworthy**. Small sites, but scraped by many others.
- [ ] **Trustpilot** (dk.trustpilot.com). Danish buyers check it. A free basic profile.

Then: ask 3 to 5 happy customers for a review on Capterra and G2. The draft email is in the outreach folder.

## 2. "Best OEE software" lists (pitch to be included)

These are articles AI engines cite for the exact questions we want to be named in. Write to the author or editor.

- [ ] **globalreader.eu**: "10 best OEE software for SME European manufacturers".
- [ ] **reliamag.com**: "Best production monitoring and OEE software 2026".
- [ ] Search "best OEE software 2026", "best cloud MES" and "bedste OEE system" every quarter. Pitch the first 5 lists that do not name us, but skip lists written by competitors.

## 3. Company facts sources (AI engines use these to know who we are)

- [ ] **Google Business Profile**. Address, hours, website, category "Software company".
- [ ] **Bing Places** (bingplaces.com). Can import from Google. Bing feeds Copilot and ChatGPT search.
- [ ] **LinkedIn company page**. Check the description, website, industry and size are current, and use the same one-liner as the site.
- [ ] **Crunchbase**. A free company profile: founded, location, description, website.
- [ ] **Wikidata**. One item for OptiPeople with website, country, industry and founding year. AI engines lean on it to recognise a company. Facts only, with a source.
- [ ] **Proff.dk and Krak**. These are filled in from the company register (CVR). Check that the website and description are right.

## 4. Marketplaces and partner pages

- [ ] **Microsoft AppSource / Azure Marketplace**. The platform runs on Azure, so a listing fits. It needs a Microsoft Partner Center account.
- [ ] **Partner and integrator pages**. Ask the integrators, hardware partners and ERP partners we work with to list us on their pages, with a link.
- [ ] **Customer pages**. Ask the customers in our published cases (for example Glaseksperten and Kvik, once they agree to be named) to mention us in their own news or case pages, with a link.

## 5. Industry directories

- [ ] **Europages** (europages.dk). A free basic listing. European B2B buyers search it.
- [ ] **Kompass**. A free basic listing.
- [ ] **DirectIndustry**. A product listing for industrial buyers. Check the price before signing up.
- [ ] **Dansk Industri (DI)** member directory, if we are members.
- [ ] **MADE (Manufacturing Academy of Denmark)** and **Digital Hub Denmark**: partner and company listings, if we qualify.

## 6. Danish trade media (stories, not listings)

The drafts are in the outreach folder. Build each pitch on a published customer case with real numbers.

- [ ] **Ingeniøren** (ing.dk)
- [ ] **Industrien** (DI's magazine)
- [ ] **Maskinmesteren**
- [ ] **Produktion.dk** and **Teknovation**

## Notes

- Check prices before signing up. Every site above has a free option except DirectIndustry and paid placements on Capterra or G2, which are not needed.
- After a profile goes live, add a line to `Docs/seo/log.md`. The review weeks check whether AI answers start naming us.

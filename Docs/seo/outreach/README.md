# Outreach drafts: get OptiPeople listed where AI engines look

Written 2026-10-26 for `targets.md` section 4. Nothing here has been sent. The human sends it.

Every claim comes from the website (case posts, module pages, `lib/structured-data.ts`). Anything not on the site is a `[bracket]` for you to fill in or delete. No invented numbers, prices or customer counts.

| File | What | Send to | Needs from you first |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `1-capterra-profile.md` | Vendor profile copy for Capterra, GetApp and Software Advice (one Gartner Digital Markets listing feeds all three) | Gartner Digital Markets vendor portal | Pricing line, founding year, logo, screenshots |
| `2-review-request-email.md` | Short review request, Danish and English | 3 to 5 customers | Pick the customers, send from a named person |
| `3-listicle-pitches.md` | Inclusion pitches to globalreader.eu and reliamag.com | Their editorial or contact address | Find the contact on each site, check the list is still open |
| `4-danish-trade-media-pitches.md` | Two story pitches, Danish | Ingeniøren/ing.dk, Industrien, Maskinmesteren | The customer's OK to be named and quoted |

## Order that works best

1. Create the profile (1), so customers have somewhere to land.
2. Send the review requests (2) the same day. Reviews take weeks to arrive.
3. Send the listicle pitches (3) once the profile is live, so you can link it.
4. Send the trade media pitches (4) after the customers have said yes.

## Rules the drafts follow

- Brand spelling: OptiPeople Data Platform. Not OptiCloud.
- Case numbers are quoted as the case posts state them, with their scope (one line, one machine, seven machines). Do not drop the scope when you edit.
- Do not offer customers money, gifts or discounts for a review. Capterra and Gartner Digital Markets reject incentivised reviews.
- Customers are told they can say anything, including criticism.

## After sending

Log the date and result in `Docs/seo/log.md`. Re-run the six Perplexity prompts in `targets.md` section 4 at the next growth loop cycle. A new listing usually takes weeks to be picked up, so a miss in the first cycle means little.

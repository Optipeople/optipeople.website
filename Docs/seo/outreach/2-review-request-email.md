# Review request to happy customers

Send to 3 to 5 customers, one at a time, from the person who knows them. Do not mass mail. A personal note gets a reply, a template blast does not.

## Who to ask

Customers with a named case and a contact who has already said something good in public:

| Customer | Contact on the case post | Why them |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dansk Træemballage | Kasper Kielgast Poulsen, plant manager | OEE up 5% in three months |
| DFI Geisler | Tommy Andersen, production manager | Seven machines, used daily for two years |
| Kvik | Stefan Lindell, lean project manager | Usage-based maintenance, clear numbers |
| Glaseksperten | `[the factory manager from the video]` | Recent (March 2026), strong quote |
| Carl Hansen & Søn | Tommy Madsen, production unit manager | Opti Assist pilot, a different story |

Check with whoever owns each account that there is no open complaint. Do not ask a customer in the middle of a problem.

## Before you send

1. Create the Capterra profile first (`1-capterra-profile.md`).
2. In the vendor portal, use the review invitation link for the product. It marks the review as verified. Paste it where the draft says `[LINK]`.
3. Fill in the `[brackets]`.

Hej [fornavn]

Tak for samarbejdet om [OEE på linjen / de syv maskiner / vedligehold efter driftstimer / maskinerne på gulvet]. Det har været godt at se, hvad I har fået ud af det.

Jeg har en lille bøn. Vi er ved at blive synlige på Capterra, hvor andre fabrikker kigger, når de skal vælge system. Dér tæller et par ærlige ord fra en, der bruger systemet hver dag, mere end noget, vi selv kan skrive.

Vil du skrive en kort anmeldelse? Det tager 5 til 10 minutter, og du kan skrive på dansk eller engelsk:

Tak på forhånd.

Venlig hilsen

## English version

**Subject:** Could you spare five minutes?

Hi [first name]

Thank you for working with us on [OEE on the line / the seven machines / maintenance on operating hours / the machines on the floor]. It has been good to see what you got out of it.

I have a small request. We are getting listed on Capterra, where other factories look when they choose a system. A few honest words from someone who uses it every day count for more than anything we can write ourselves.

Would you write a short review? It takes 5 to 10 minutes, in English or Danish:

[LINK]

Write what you actually think, including what we could do better. That is what readers trust, and we can use it.

We offer nothing in return, and you are under no obligation. If you would rather I ask someone else at your company, just say.

Thank you.

Best regards
[name]
[title], OptiPeople
[phone]

## Follow up

One reminder after 7 to 10 days, two lines, no pressure. Then drop it.

## Short description (about 300 characters)

OptiPeople Data Platform connects the machines a factory already has, including older ones, and shows OEE, stops and shift performance live. Operators register stop causes at the machine. Managers get the same numbers across lines and shifts. Modules for quality, maintenance, energy and production orders are switched on one at a time.

## Full description

OptiPeople Data Platform is a modular platform that connects the shopfloor to the business systems around it. It reads data from the machines, PLCs and systems a factory already runs, and puts one live view in front of operators and managers.

**Start with OEE.** The OEE module measures availability, performance and quality straight from the machines, per machine, line and shift, without handwritten lists or spreadsheets. Operators choose the stop cause on a screen at the machine while the stop happens, so the stop data is the operators' own.

**Add modules when they matter.** The platform is a modular MES. The modules are Planning, Orders, Quality, IoT, OEE, Energy (EMS), Maintenance, Documents and AI Agents. A site starts with the one that hurts most and adds the rest later.

**Connects to what you have.** Open integration through OPC-UA, MQTT, REST API or direct PLC integration. New sensors are only added where there is nothing to read from. The Orders module works standalone and syncs two ways with an ERP if the customer wants it.

**Built for the shopfloor.** Live OEE dashboards for large screens, operator views on tablets, weekly machine reports, shift performance and stop analysis.

**Assistant and agents.** Opti Assist answers questions about machines and production data in plain language, and AI agents watch for patterns in the customer's own data.

Customers named on the site: Dansk Træemballage, DFI Geisler, Kvik, Glaseksperten, Carl Hansen & Søn, Dansand, Expedit.
